# Brief Bio of John MacCormick

John MacCormick has degrees in mathematics from the University of Cambridge and the University of Auckland, and a doctorate in computer vision from the University of Oxford.  

He was a research fellow at Linacre College, Oxford (1999–2000), a research scientist at HP Labs (2000–2003), and a computer scientist with Microsoft Research (2003–2007).  

Since 2007, Dr. MacCormick has been a professor of computer science at Dickinson College. He is the author of four books, including *Nine Algorithms That Changed the Future: The Ingenious Ideas That Drive Today's Computers* (which has been translated into eight languages), and *Thinking AI: How Artificial Intelligence Emulates Human Understanding* (forthcoming in 2026).  

MacCormick has filed over a dozen US patents on novel computer technologies and is the author of numerous peer-reviewed academic conference and journal papers. His work spans several sub-fields of computer science, including computer vision, large-scale distributed systems, computer science education, and the public understanding of computer science and artificial intelligence.
